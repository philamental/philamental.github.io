### **Chameleons**

We are chameleons
you and I
dancing through our lifetimes
becoming what others want to see
when they peer into our eyes
we are not fakers,
we are Becomers
for we truly become something different
each and every time the landscape changes

A rose by any other name
may still be a rose,
but it also takes on the features of the Name
to whomever has given it that name
Blame us not for what we are;
it is not something that we asked for
we have changed from what we once were
full of pain and despair and loneliness
into something bold and sleek and powerful...
or soft and demure and beguiling
but not with any intention
other than to satisfy the unspoken expectations
of those who cannot or will not change themselves

As fluid as mercury and as solid as marble
we take a shape, a form, feeling
and mold it carefully, refining the details
until the form has a life of its own
then we freeze it solid
and peer outward from behind our eyes
observing the observers
noting their expressions and reactions
until they glance away for a moment
so that we can refine ourselves once more

In the end, there can be no permanency for us
for we are by definition That Which Changes
and having reached a point of perfection
that should stand the test of time,
we have no choice but to change ourselves again

We are chameleons, you and I
Recognize me while you can

                     -Human Author
