Today…

     I will be silent Phil

Quiet pensive moments
    drift like a trickling stream
           from the spring of my existence

I watch them
     as they seep up 
             from the ground
       in the tiniest of droplets

   …gathering together

           joining as they meet

 to become more
             than they were
                    a moment earlier
     until they attain
         enough mass to begin 
                 the long journey downward 
                       into the Surface World

They are given no choice
          as to whether or not
          they want to make this journey,
   through these wet & slippery
   moments in time - 

They are not concerned
        that they were never given a choice –
     the point is moot


They will come forth
     from the pool which forms
          from the fabric of the Universe
        and begin the downhill pilgrimage
            back to the Ocean of Time
                       from whence they came

When they finally arrive,
         tired and excited and renewed
               all at the same time,

    they will dive back down
          as deep as they can possibly go


work their way 
        back into the ground
     and come springing up

                pure and clear and fresh

             into somebody else’s reality

				-Phil  12/13/21
